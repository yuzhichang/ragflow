- When a semantic sweep returns category-off hits, re-describe the target by artifact form and era instead of rephrasing the concept, and rerun once
- Cap two probes per clue session-wide; when a candidate fails twice, retire the clue instead of generating new candidates or re-reading long documents
- Batch all clue hops into one concurrent wave, one lexical plus one semantic probe per hop, and never widen beyond that wave
- Batch independent clue chains as concurrent queries, cap each at two candidates and one quote, and defer final acceptance to mandatory-clause checks
- Require proof only for distinguishing clauses; once one candidate covers them, answer and name any unconfirmed corroborating clause
- Stop as soon as the answer clause and each spouse clause have one supporting quote; name remaining gaps instead of searching further
- Build a synonym family per clue before searching, and probe one representative of each family in parallel rather than committing to a single literal term
- Accept birth-year and birthplace facts from separate passages naming the same relative; reserve single-passage quotation for the answer's own wording
- Never enumerate candidate rosters; keep only the single candidate satisfying every clause and discard the rest once one confirming passage exists
- For verbatim-text questions, prefer canonical or official sources and audio-derived transcriptions over fan pages, verify final wording in a second retrieved passage, and never fill gaps from memory
