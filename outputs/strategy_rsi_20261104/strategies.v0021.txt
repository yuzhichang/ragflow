- Run cheap lexical retrieval before semantic retrieval, batch clue clusters into one parallel query, cap semantic calls at one, discard topic-only hits, and stop when one chunk confirms two clues
- Anchor probes on distinctive literal phrases, genres, and quoted titles instead of inventing concrete title names to chase relationships
- Batch independent clue probes into one parallel multi-query call; if subagents are unavailable, run concurrently, and confirm both required chains before a two-clue match
- Stop searching before most of the budget is spent; rank survivors and write a partial answer naming unconfirmed links
- Timebox each clue to two probes, retire misses, cap survivors at three, and stop when one passage confirms two clues; answer best candidate naming unconfirmed links
- Grep candidate quotes literally beside dates; for ordinal clues, retrieve structured ranked lists, intersect positions numerically, and never paraphrase wording
- Confirm each multi-hop link in its own passage; cap clue chains at two probes, retire misses, and require one passage for final attribution
- After two failed probes, retire the clause, rank survivors by confirmed quotes, and stop without expanding or inventing names
- Run independent clue chains as concurrent queries in one batched call, cap each at two candidates and one quote, and accept the first two-clue confirmation
- Bootstrap the entity from biography clues in one document, then validate discography clues only after it names the artist
