- Order clue beats by distinctiveness, merge constraints per candidate, batch short queries, filter other clues in the resolving document, and defer new topics until the rarest clause fails
- Run cheap lexical retrieval before semantic retrieval, cap semantic calls at one, and discard semantic hits that match only the topic
- Search distinctive tokens only, capped at a few probes per candidate, and abandon a candidate name that yields no hits after the first targeted query
- If a non-hard numeric or timing clue remains unverifiable, leave it unresolved, answer from verified names, and rerank only existing candidates without widening
- Anchor on rarest supported clause, skip absent numeric clauses and broad dates, require one document to confirm two attributes, then stop; abandon failing candidates and rerank only by unverifiable distances
- When one passage lists several titles by the same author, extract every candidate verbatim, choose with the separating clue, and stop after one targeted miss without broadening
- Treat asserted distances or adjacency as hard filters requiring passage support, not unverified peripheral details
- List up to three key-clause candidates, probe each with a parallel subagent anchored on the rarest clue, and stop when one document confirms two attributes
- If a multi-term pattern fails, grep the resolved title once, run one semantic re-description, then probe one fallback title and stop
- When identifying an organization's members, exclude credits affiliated with publishers, contractors, or external vendors even if their names appear on multiple titles
