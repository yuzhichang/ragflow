- After one semantic and one keyword sweep per clue, stop rephrasing; convert date constraints into month-day and year windows and search those
- Cap total probes across all clues, launch independent chains concurrently in one wave, and retire failed clues instead of re-reading
- Require proof only for distinguishing clauses; once one candidate covers them, answer and name any unconfirmed corroborating clause
- Launch clue chains in one parallel wave; pin the most discriminative numeric clause before dependent hops, verify separating clauses, accept one passage, name gaps, and retire after two misses
- Give each parallel subagent three probes; require one candidate plus one verbatim quote, accepting only candidates satisfying the discriminating date and relationship clauses together
- Track clause-to-passage coverage separately; accept when discriminative clauses pin one candidate and remaining clauses each have confirming passages
- Never enumerate candidate rosters; keep only the single candidate satisfying every clause and discard the rest once one confirming passage exists
- Launch clue chains in one parallel wave; cap each clue at two synonym probes per mode, probe one representative per family, then pivot to entity pages and retire empties
- Accept birth-year and birthplace facts from separate passages naming the same relative; reserve single-passage quotation for the answer's own wording
- For verbatim-text questions, prefer canonical or audio-derived sources; verify wording, line boundaries, and numbering in a second passage, never filling gaps from memory
