- Order clues by distinctiveness, merge constraints per candidate, and resolve multi-hop chains link by link from the rarest literal term, confirming each hop before the next topic
- Run cheap lexical retrieval before semantic retrieval, batch clue clusters into one parallel query, cap semantic calls at one, discard topic-only hits, and stop when one chunk confirms two clues
- Route independent clue pairs to parallel subagents in round one; accept the first two-clue confirmation and read its document fully for totals, then stop
- Do not answer while hard clues remain unverified; widen through resolved-document leads, but keep confirmed names, state the unresolved link, and stop if one targeted link resists
- After two failed candidate probes, mine the resolved document's related-title list for the next lead instead of inventing names; rerank only existing candidates
- Treat adjacency as final verification, not a candidate filter: accept a two-attribute candidate whose document co-locates anchor and target on one page, then stop
- Time-box candidate generation to two rounds; probe at most three candidates once; anchor on rarest verbatim clue, read that document, accept two co-located attributes, stop at two hard clues
- When a clue phrase is generic, skip semantic re-description; probe the two rarest clauses separately and require one document confirming two clues
- Route independent clue chains to parallel subagents; each single-clue probe returns at most two candidates with a confirming quote, then join and stop at two hard clues
- When one passage lists several titles by the same author, extract every candidate verbatim, choose with the separating clue, and stop after one targeted miss without broadening
